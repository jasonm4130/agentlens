# Taking part in an agentlens test session

**What this is.** agentlens is an open-source tool that tries to tell people apart from automated software (AI agents, browser automation) by how a web page is used. To check that it never mistakes people for software, including people who use a trackpad, a phone, a keyboard only, a screen reader, voice control, dictation or an input method, we ask people to do a short task on a test page while it runs.

**What you would do.** Use a test page on [the computer or phone you normally use / a computer we provide] for about 5 to 10 minutes: search, open an article, scroll, fill in a short form with made-up details we give you, and click a few buttons. Use it however you normally would, with any tools you normally use. There are no right or wrong ways.

**What is recorded.** Only what the tool itself keeps: counts and timing summaries, such as how many clicks and key presses there were and roughly how long they took, sorted into broad ranges. It does **not** record what you type, where on the screen you click, your screen, your voice, the websites you visit, or your name. We note which kind of input you used (for example "mouse" or "screen reader on iPhone") and give your session a code such as P03 instead of your name.

**Where it goes.** Your session's summary counts, under your code, may be published in the project's public repository on GitHub as test data, and the overall results (for example "0 of 12 screen-reader sessions were flagged") may appear in its documentation. Only [name of the person running the session] knows which code is yours, and that link is kept [where] and never published.

**It is your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during the session without giving a reason, and nothing from an unfinished session is kept unless you say it can be.

**Taking it back later.** You can ask us to delete your sessions at any time by contacting [contact], quoting your code. We will delete them from our files and from the current version of the public repository within [14] days and tell you when it is done. Copies already published may remain in the repository's history and in copies other people have made, so if that matters to you, ask us before [date] and we will hold your sessions back from publication until then.

**Questions.** Ask [name] now or later at [contact].
